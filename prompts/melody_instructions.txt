[dwell_notes]

I chose these notes motivated by the key signature, style of music, and feeling of the piece. The piece is intended to dwell on these notes the most, listed in that order of importance and repetition, for example the weights might be (0.5, 0.25, 0.125, 0.125). In addition, imagine the following connections between notes: A-A, A-B1, B1-B2, A-B2, A-C1, C1-C2, A-C2, B1-C1, B2-C2. Each of these "edges" is a sequence of elements with durations scaled to 1 being a single beat based on the time signature. An element can be one of the following:

* A rapid passage like `C5>Bb4>G4>C5`

Here are the edges I have constructed to be used the next step for creating the melody:
[melodic_edges]

Follow these execution steps exactly when building the final melody:
1. Insert 2 whole measures of `rest` at the very beginning, then start copying edges.
2. When you follow an edge, copy every row from that edge (element + duration) in order and without simplification. The only thing you are allowed to change is the `measure` number so the sequence stays continuous.
3. Never collapse an edge into long sustained dwell notes, invent alternative notes, or skip rows. The melody should sound like the provided edges stitched together.
4. After finishing one edge you "arrive" on that edge's destination dwell note. Choose the next edge that begins on that note (you may run the next edge forward or backward by reversing its order). Continue chaining edges until you reach the target length, touching every edge at least once overall.
5. Only after the melody reaches the desired length (120-150 measures) append 2 whole measures of `rest` to end cleanly.

* Do not add any new material besides the mandated rest measures; every musical note must come from the provided edges.
* After following an edge, you "arrive" at the next dwell note, and then choose the next edge beginning at that note.
